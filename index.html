Our Story

A Brooklyn corner,
run like family

Torres Wines & Spirits isn't a chain and never will be. We're a family-owned shop on Fulton Street, built on the simple idea that buying a good bottle should feel personal.

Walk in and you'll be met by people who actually know the shelves — happy to point you toward the perfect bourbon, the right wine for dinner, or something new you haven't tried. No pressure, no upsell, just honest guidance.

Our neighbors keep coming back for the selection, the prices, and the welcome. Some even leave with a little treat.

— The Torres Family
